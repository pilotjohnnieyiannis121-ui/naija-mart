How Naija Mart works

  1. DiscoverFind products and shops by item, market or state.
  2. CompareCheck price, location and stock.
  3. ContactMessage the seller on Naija Mart.
  4. ArrangeAgree payment, pickup or delivery with the seller.

Good to know: Naija Mart helps you find and contact sellers. It doesn’t take payment or guarantee delivery. A Verified badge means we reviewed the seller’s details, not that a sale is guaranteed. Read our safety guidelines before paying anyone.
