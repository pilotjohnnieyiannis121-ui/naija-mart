Questions

Good to know

Does Naija Mart process payments or deliver goods?

No. Naija Mart helps you find products and contact sellers. You agree payment, pickup or delivery directly with the seller. Never pay in advance to someone you can’t verify.

How do sellers get approved?

Every seller applies with their details. Our team reviews each application, and only approved sellers can publish products.

What does the Verified badge mean?

It means our team reviewed that seller’s details. It is separate from Premium membership and is not a guarantee of any sale or product.

Can I buy and sell with the same account?

Yes. Create one account, then apply to sell from your account page. After approval you can use both.

How do I report a suspicious listing or person?

Use the report option on a product or profile. Reports are private and reviewed by our team.
